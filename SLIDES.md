## Slide 2 — The Problem

AI agents now need live public web data. But unmanaged agents are risky.

- Hit unauthorized or unsafe domains
- Burn proxy and API budgets with no limits
- Ingest hidden prompt injections from web pages
- Produce business claims with no source citations
- Leave no audit trail for compliance

Enterprises cannot let agents browse freely.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

**WebSentry AI is a governed control plane for live-web AI agents.**

Every investigation passes through a policy engine before evidence reaches the LLM:

- Approved-domain policy enforcement
- Budget limits and spend telemetry
- Bright Data product routing
- Page-level prompt-injection scanning
- Full step-by-step audit log

---

## Slide 4 — How It Works

1. User defines target, competitors, focus areas, domain policy, budget, risk tolerance
2. **Bright Data SERP API** discovers pricing, hiring, launch, and positioning sources
3. Policy engine allows approved domains, blocks unsafe results
4. **Bright Data Web Unlocker** fetches approved pages
5. Each page scanned as untrusted content for hidden instructions
6. **AI/ML API** generates a citation-backed GTM intelligence brief

---

## Slide 5 — Bright Data Is Load-Bearing

- **SERP API** powers real-time discovery of public sources
- **Web Unlocker** provides reliable public page access
- The UI presents the full workflow as an observable agent tool timeline
- If credentials are unavailable during judging, deterministic demo mode preserves the exact same policy, risk, audit, and brief flow

---

## Slide 6 — The Safety Layer

WebSentry treats every fetched page as untrusted content.

- Detects hidden prompt-injection fixtures
- Strips unsafe instructions before evidence reaches the model
- Scores risk per investigation
- Blocks unapproved domains and unsafe mirror results

This is the enterprise layer that makes web agents safe to deploy.

---

## Slide 7 — Business Output

- Pricing signals

- Source citations for every claim

Exportable as audit JSON.
